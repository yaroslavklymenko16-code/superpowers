Questions parents ask

Does my kid need to know how to code?

No. Mission 1 starts from opening Roblox Studio for the first time. If they can type and follow steps, they're ready. Best for ages 9–14.

What do we need?

A Windows PC or Mac (Roblox Studio doesn't run on phones, tablets or Chromebooks), the free Roblox Studio app, a Roblox account for your kid, and a Claude account for you. Claude's free plan is enough to get started.

Isn't this just more screen time?

It's screen time where they make something. The included Creator Hours chart helps you set a deal, like "an hour of building earns thirty minutes of playing," so time spent creating counts differently from time spent playing.

Is the AI safe for kids?

Your kid uses Claude with you, on your account. Mission 6 teaches the safety rules directly: never share personal info, check what AI says, and remember AI is a tool. You stay in the room for every session.

What format is it?

A 13-page printable PDF workbook with every script, every prompt, a parent guide and a certificate. Download it instantly after purchase.
