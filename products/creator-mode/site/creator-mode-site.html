Questions parents ask

Does my kid need to know how to code?

No. Mission 1 starts with opening Roblox Studio for the first time. If they can read and follow steps, they're ready. Best for ages 9–14.

What do we need?

A Windows PC or Mac (Roblox Studio doesn't run on phones, tablets or Chromebooks), the free Roblox Studio app, a Roblox account for your kid, and a Claude account for you. The free Claude plan works to start; a paid plan is smoother for long sessions because the scripts are big.

What exactly do I download?

A zip with Creator Quest (opens in any web browser, with 8 big games and 21 skill quests), the Creator Vault (118 Roblox model files), rescue scripts for every mission, and a 4-page parent guide.

Isn't this just more screen time?

It's screen time where they make something. Skill quests take 10–15 minutes, so they fit a school night. The parent guide includes a Creator Hours chart so you can agree on a deal, like "an hour of building earns thirty minutes of playing."

Is the AI safe for kids?

Claude runs on your account with you in the room. Mission 6 teaches the safety rules directly: never share personal info, check what AI says, and remember AI is a tool, not a friend.
