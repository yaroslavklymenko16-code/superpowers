# Creator Mode: ads

Audience: **parents** (not kids) of 9–14 year olds who play a lot of Roblox.
Promise: turn Roblox time into building time, and learn AI together.
Offer: $29 one-time PDF course. Send ad clicks to the checkout/product page.

## Rules that keep the ads approved

- **Target parents only.** Never target or address children. Meta and TikTok restrict ads aimed at under-18s, and kids can't buy anyway.
- **Don't claim to know the viewer's situation.** Meta rejects ads that assert personal traits ("Your kid is addicted"). Use "Roblox-obsessed kid?" questions and general statements instead.
- **No Roblox logo, characters, screenshots of other people's games or Roblox fonts.** Say "Roblox" only to describe what the course is for. Same for Claude/Anthropic: no logos, no hint of endorsement.
- **No made-up reviews, numbers or "regular price" discounts.** Add real testimonials only once real families send them.
- **No "addiction" or medical claims.** Say "screen time", not "Roblox addiction".

### Meta setup (Ads Manager)
- Objective: **Sales** (or **Traffic** if the Meta Pixel isn't on your checkout page yet).
- Location: start with your own country only (US/UK/CA/AU are typical).
- Age 28–50, all genders. Detailed targeting (if available in your account): *Parents with preteens (8–12 years)*, *Parents with teenagers (13–17)*, plus interests *Roblox*, *Coding for kids*, *STEM education*, *Homeschooling*. If those aren't offered, use Advantage+ audience with the age range above.
- Placements: Advantage+ placements.
- Budget: **$20/day** split across Ads A–D. Run 3–4 days before judging.
- Kill an ad if: cost per click is over $1.50 after ~1,000 impressions, or no sales after ~$40 spent on it. Move budget to the winner.

## TikTok (parents scroll here too)

- Format: 15–25s vertical video, filmed on a phone. A parent's voice works best.
- Script:
  1. (0–3s, kid on Roblox) "My son plays Roblox for hours."
  2. (3–8s, Studio open, kid typing into Claude) "So we tried something. Instead of playing games, he's building one, with an AI helper."
  3. (8–15s, lava floor works, kid reacts) "First hour, he made a floor that's lava. He's learning real code, and how AI actually works."
  4. (15–20s, workbook on screen) "It's called Creator Mode. Seven missions, we do them together. Link's in the bio."
- Ad text: Turning Roblox time into building time 🧱 7-mission parent + kid course, ages 9–14
- Targeting: age 25–54, interests *Parenting*, *Education*, *Video games*. $20/day.
- Film the real session. Don't fake reactions.
