# Creator Mode: ads

Audience: **parents** (not kids) of 9–14 year olds who play a lot of Roblox.
Promise: turn Roblox time into building time, and learn AI together.
Offer: $29 one-time download: Creator Quest (a Duolingo-style path: 8 big games + 21 skill quests, XP, streaks, badges), 118 Roblox 3D models, rescue scripts, parent guide. Send ad clicks to the checkout/product page.

## Rules that keep the ads approved

- **Target parents only.** Never target or address children. Meta and TikTok restrict ads aimed at under-18s, and kids can't buy anyway.
- **Don't claim to know the viewer's situation.** Meta rejects ads that assert personal traits ("Your kid is addicted"). Use "Roblox-obsessed kid?" questions and general statements instead.
- **No Roblox logo, characters, screenshots of other people's games or Roblox fonts.** Say "Roblox" only to describe what the course is for. Same for Claude/Anthropic: no logos, no hint of endorsement.
- **No made-up reviews, numbers or "regular price" discounts.** Add real testimonials only once real families send them.
- **No "addiction" or medical claims.** Say "screen time", not "Roblox addiction".
