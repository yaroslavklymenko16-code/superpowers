## Description (paste into Gumroad)

**Stop playing Roblox. Start building it.**

Creator Quest is a game-like course where your kid makes real Roblox games with AI. They describe what they want, and Claude builds it directly inside Roblox Studio, tests it and fixes its own bugs. Your kid is the designer and the tester. You're the co-pilot.

**What they'll build**

- 🌋 **Lava Tower:** a 25-stage obby with spinning lasers and checkpoints
- 🪙 **Coin Rush:** collectibles, a shop and a Gold Rain event
- 🏭 **Tycoon Factory:** droppers, conveyors and upgrades
- 🧟 **Zombie Night:** swords, zombie waves and giant zombies
- 🥚 **Pet Hatchery:** mystery eggs, rarities and pets that follow you
- ⏱️ **Round Arena:** a lobby, countdown and minigame rounds
- 🔥 **Boss Battle:** a giant boss with attack phases and a health bar
- ⭐ **Dream Game:** their own idea, published on Roblox

**Feels like a game, not homework**

- A Duolingo-style path: each step unlocks the next
- 21 short skill quests (10–15 min) between the big games: 3D models, tools, NPCs, special effects and more
- XP, levels, daily streaks and badges
- Pictures of Roblox Studio showing exactly where to click
- A new pack of 3D models unlocks after every game
- Profiles for brothers and sisters

**They learn how AI works, too**

Every game teaches one big idea: how AI predicts, why it makes mistakes, how to give clear instructions, how to check its work, and how to stay safe online. An "Explain it" step after each game makes sure they understand what got built.

**What you get**

- **Creator Quest**: opens right in your browser on PC or Mac. Nothing to install or unzip.
- **Creator Vault**: 118 ready-made Roblox models in 10 packs, built into the app (46 work as soon as you press Play)
- **Backup scripts** for every big game, so nobody gets stuck
- **Parent guide** (6 pages): setup, safety tips and troubleshooting

**What you need**

- A Windows PC or Mac (Roblox Studio doesn't run on phones, tablets or Chromebooks)
- Roblox Studio (free) and a Roblox account for your kid
- The Claude Desktop app on your Claude account. Long builds work best on a paid Claude plan.
- No coding experience. A warm-up unit walks you through every step of setup.

**Built for doing together**

Claude is for users 18 and over, so Creator Quest runs on your Claude account with you beside your kid. They pick what to build and test it. You send the prompts and approve each step. It's screen time you do together, and they make something real.
