The co-pilot rule

Claude's terms require users to be 18 or older. So Creator Quest is built for you and your kid together: Claude runs on your account, on your screen, with you in the room. Your kid reads the mission, edits the prompt, does the building in Roblox Studio and the testing. You paste prompts into Claude and copy the answers back. At the end of each mission, you hold the "stamp" button to complete it.

What you need

  • A Windows PC or Mac. Roblox Studio doesn't run on phones, tablets or Chromebooks.
  • Roblox Studio, free from create.roblox.com.
  • A Roblox account for your kid. Turn on Parental Controls for under-13s.
  • A Claude account for you at claude.ai.

Free or paid Claude?

The free plan works for getting started. The Mega Prompts ask Claude for long scripts (150–300 lines), so on the free plan you may hit the daily message limit after a mission or two. A paid plan makes longer sessions smoother. Either way, the rescue scripts mean you're never stuck.

Each mission has 5 stages

1. AI BrainA short lesson about how AI works, plus a 2-question quiz. Read it together.
2. BuildSetup checklist, the Mega Prompt (one prompt that makes Claude write a whole game system), and a "test it" checklist.
3. Boss BugA short piece of real code with one bug. Your kid taps the broken line.
4. Power-upsExtra prompts that add features. Kids pick what sounds fun.
5. FinishA conversation question for you, then you hold the stamp button.

When things go wrong

Troubleshooting

ProblemFix
Nothing happens when we press PlayCheck the script is inside ServerScriptService (not Workspace) and that it's a Script, not a LocalScript. Open View › Output and look for red text.
Red error in OutputCopy the whole red line plus the script and send both to Claude: "Here's my error: ... Here's my script: ... What's wrong?"
Claude's answer got cut offType "continue". Or ask "Give me the full script again in one piece."
We've tried three fixes and it's still brokenUse the rescue script from the Build stage (or the Rescue Scripts folder). Then ask Claude to add your changes on top of it.
Coins don't save (Mission 2)Saving needs a published game. File › Publish to Roblox, then Home › Game Settings › Security › turn on "Enable Studio Access to API Services".
Two scripts are fightingEach mission should be its own place. Start a new Baseplate for each one.
Insert from File doesn't show the Vault filesUnzip the download first. Then right-click Workspace › Insert from File and browse to the Creator Vault folder.
Creator Quest progress disappearedProgress is saved in the browser you used. Use the same browser on the same computer, and don't use a private window.

Online safety

  • Publish games as Private or Friends only at first (Game Settings on create.roblox.com).
  • Keep Roblox Parental Controls on for under-13s.
  • Mission 6 teaches never to share full name, school, address or passwords with AI or in game chat.
  • Claude stays on your account. Your kid doesn't need their own.
